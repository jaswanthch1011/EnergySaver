    One extra unit can cost more than the last hundred.

    Most Indian households pay on slabs: the more units you use, the more each additional unit costs. A small, unnoticed rise in usage can cross a boundary, and you only find out when the bill arrives.

    EnergySaver watches your running total, projects where the month will end, and warns you days before a slab is crossed.

    From a plug to a plain-language tip, all on your phone.

    Set up takes a few minutes per appliance. After that, EnergySaver works quietly in the background.

    1. 1

      Plug in and pair

      Put a metering smart plug between the socket and the appliance. It joins your home Wi-Fi and the app finds it on the local network. No account to create.

    2. 2

      Log every reading

      The plug reports power in watts every few seconds. EnergySaver stores the readings in a database on your phone and keeps the history even when the internet is down.

    3. 3

      Learn what is normal

      Lightweight statistical models recognise each appliance by its power signature, learn its usual load, and project your month-end units and bill from your tariff slabs.

    4. 4

      Advise, then act

      A small language model on the phone turns the numbers into advice. Tap once to cut power to an idle device, schedule the geyser, or set an off-peak reminder. A running counter shows what each action saved.

    Every appliance has a power signature.

    A fridge breathes in short cycles. A geyser is one tall block. EnergySaver matches these shapes to name the appliance behind each plug and notices when a familiar shape changes.

    Shapes are schematic. You confirm each appliance's name once, and the app learns from your corrections.

    Questions

    Which smart plugs work?

    EnergySaver is designed for Wi-Fi plugs with built-in energy metering that report readings over the local network, such as plugs with a local API or ones running open firmware. Cloud-only plugs that offer no local access are not a fit.

    Does it need the internet?

    No. Monitoring, forecasting, anomaly detection and the coach all run on the phone. You only need a connection to install or update the app.

    How does it know my tariff?

    You enter your slab rates once. Pulling the exact tariff and billing data from your DISCOM is on the roadmap.

    Can the AI get the numbers wrong?

    The language model does not calculate. Costs, baselines and forecasts come from statistical models, and the language model only phrases them. That keeps the advice grounded in your readings.

    Which appliances should I start with?

    Begin with the biggest or most forgotten loads: the AC, the geyser, and the set-top box. Add plugs as you want more detail.

    Will it work on my phone?

    EnergySaver targets Android phones. The language model is quantized small enough for mid-range devices, though responses are faster on newer chips.
